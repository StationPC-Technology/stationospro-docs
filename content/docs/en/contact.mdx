If you need help, have questions about features, or want to share feedback while using the product, reach us through the channels below and we will provide support promptly.

## Official Platforms
- Website: visit the [StationPC website](https://www.stationpc.com) for the latest product news
- Support email: write to support@stationpc.com and we will reply within 2 business days
- Community: visit the [StationPC Community](https://www.stationpc.com/community/forum) for release notes, feature roadmaps, and FAQ, to share tips and use cases, and to discuss product-related topics

## Technical Discussion and Feedback
Discord discussion group: join the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/R9acnGtDnP) to discuss issues in real time with the official technical team and other users, share your experience, submit feedback directly, and get faster technical support.

Zhongshan Yinghuo Technology Co., Ltd. ("we" or "us") provides NAS system software, clients, web administration, remote connections, and related services through StationHub for families, teams, and businesses. This Policy explains the boundaries, purposes, and methods of our processing. Feature fields and permissions are further described in the [Personal Information Processing List](./personal-information-processing-list), [Permission Request and Usage Statement](./permission-usage-statement), and [Third-Party Personal Information Sharing List](./third-party-sdk-list).

If you use StationHub for a family, school, company, or other organization, confirm that you are authorized and tell members about accounts, shared content, administrator access, and audit arrangements in the organization space.

## 1. Scope

This Policy covers StationHub NAS devices and systems, mobile and desktop clients, web administration, account services, device discovery, remote management, updates, notifications, technical support, and other features that refer to this Policy.

Third-party apps, containers, plugins, cloud drives, models, and external services that you install on a NAS are operated independently by their providers. Review their privacy policies and permission notices before using them.

StationHub supports local and online operation. Without cloud features, many management and file operations can stay on the local network. Remote connections, cloud accounts, notifications, updates, the app center, and support may send information over a network.

## 2. Information we process

We process information needed for each feature. Common categories include the following; the Processing List and feature pages control the actual fields.

### 2.1 Account and membership information

Email address, phone number where supported, verification codes, password summaries, account ID, nickname, avatar, organization ID, member role, device ownership, and account-security settings. Invitations, removals, and permission changes also create relationship and operation records.

### 2.2 Device, network, and operation information

Device serial number, model, system and firmware version, device name, online status, storage and network summaries, client type, IP address, region or country, remote-connection status, and technical details needed for discovery, warranty, updates, and remote management.

### 2.3 Security, diagnostics, and support information

Login time and method, device and browser information, failed authentication, permission changes, remote-access records, app installation, system updates, errors, and crash logs. Support, ticket, repair, and troubleshooting requests may also include contact details, descriptions, screenshots, diagnostic logs, device configuration, and files you submit.

### 2.4 Optional-feature information

When you enable remote access, cloud sync, push notifications, the app center, backups, third-party drives, containers, or AI services, we may process tokens, domains, task status, file paths, thumbnails, model or app names, and transfer logs. The third party’s rules and the permissions you grant determine what it actually reads.

### 2.5 NAS file content

During local management, LAN access, and use without cloud features, photos, documents, databases, backups, contacts, and other files generally remain on your NAS or endpoint. We do not automatically read, copy, or upload them in the background. If you enable remote access, sync, search, thumbnails, AI, or support, related content may be handled as the feature explains.

## 3. Why we process information

We use information to:

- provide registration, sign-in, device binding, member management, access control, and NAS management;
- establish remote connections, send device alerts, security notices, and updates, and provide discovery, warranty, and support;
- detect suspicious sign-ins, abuse, malware, and attacks and maintain necessary security audit records;
- analyze crashes and performance and improve system, client, and service stability; and
- answer inquiries, complaints, and rights requests and comply with contracts, laws, courts, and regulators.

Optional product analytics and experience research are presented in the relevant context and require consent where required. We do not use information for unrelated purposes; if a purpose changes, we will notify you and obtain consent where required.

## 4. Local accounts, cloud accounts, and organizations

4.1. Local offline-account data is mainly stored on the user’s NAS hardware. When cloud functions are disabled, our servers do not obtain file content merely because you browse or manage files on the NAS.

4.2. Information needed for cloud accounts, remote connections, notifications, updates, and support may be stored on servers operated by us or service providers. To provide services across regions, necessary information may be transferred outside your country or region with safeguards required by law, such as encryption, de-identification, access control, contractual restrictions, or other measures.

4.3. A company or organization generally determines the purpose and method of processing member data. Administrators may manage accounts, shared spaces, apps, backups, and audit logs under organization policy. Members should first ask the administrator to access, correct, export, or delete information in the organization space.

## 5. Sharing, processors, and third-party connections

5.1. We do not sell personal information. To provide hosting, messaging, support, troubleshooting, storage, network security, or remote connections, we may provide necessary information to bound service providers and require them to process it only for agreed purposes.

5.2. We use SDKs and other third-party components within the scope listed in this Policy and the [Third-Party Personal Information Sharing List](./third-party-sdk-list). A component may run locally or send device, diagnostic, or network information to its provider; the list gives the relevant fields, purpose, and policy links.

5.3. Third-party apps, drives, containers, and AI services that you connect process data under the permissions you grant. Providing an integration entry does not make us the controller of that third party’s data, and we are not responsible for its privacy practices, availability, or results.

5.4. We may disclose information when required for law, court or regulatory process, user or public safety, infringement complaints, or our lawful rights. For a merger, acquisition, asset transfer, or change of service entity, we will give notice as required and require the recipient to continue appropriate protections.

## 6. Retention, security, and deletion

6.1. We retain information for as long as needed for the processing purpose. After account closure, device unbinding, organization closure, or withdrawal of an optional authorization, we delete, anonymize, or stop processing information unless retention is needed for law, disputes, security audits, or backup recovery. Backup copies may be overwritten after a reasonable period.

6.2. We use authentication, access controls, transport and storage protections, least privilege, audit logs, vulnerability fixes, backups, and personnel controls to reduce risk. No Internet, endpoint, or user network is perfectly secure. Use strong passwords, available multi-factor authentication, few administrators, and current software.

6.3. If a security incident may affect your rights, we will take remedial measures under applicable law and notify affected users or organizations through in-app notices, email, announcements, or other appropriate channels.

## 7. Managing your information

Subject to applicable law and service conditions, you may:

- view, copy, or correct account, member, and device information;
- manage notifications, system permissions, remote access, device analytics, and marketing subscriptions;
- request account deletion, device unbinding, cloud-data deletion, or withdrawal of optional authorization;
- request export of data you are entitled to export from a personal account or organization space; and
- ask questions or request restrictions regarding automated processing, cross-border transfer, or third-party connections.

Personal-account settings are available in StationHub account or device settings. Organization members should contact the administrator first. We may verify identity, account ownership, or organizational authority before processing a request, and may decline or delay a request for legal, safety, rights-protection, or dispute reasons while explaining the applicable reason.

## 8. Minors and family members

StationHub is mainly for adults and organizational users. Minors should use it with a parent’s or legal guardian’s consent and guidance. A family-device owner must not collect, share, or publish a minor’s personal information without the necessary authorization. If you believe we processed a minor’s information without guardian consent, contact us using the details below.

## 9. Updates and contact

We may update this Policy as law, technology, or services change. We will describe material changes on the page, in the app, by email, or through another appropriate channel. Where law requires renewed consent, we will obtain it as required. Continued use after publication means acceptance of the update unless law provides otherwise.

For questions, requests, or complaints about this Policy, personal-information processing, or data security, email service@spcnasos.com. We will handle requests within the period required by applicable law. You may complain to the data-protection authority in your jurisdiction if you are not satisfied with our response.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. This policy explains how StationPC Technology Co., Limited ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects your personal information when you use StationPC App, web portal, PC‑Mac clients and related hardware‑software services.

This Privacy Policy is an integral part of the User Agreement. By accessing and using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and accept all contents of this Privacy Policy.

Related reference documents:

- [Personal Information Processing List](./personal-information-processing-list)
- [Permission Request and Usage Statement](./permission-usage-statement)

## 1. Scope of Application

This Privacy Policy applies to StationPC portable NAS hardware, mobile App, web management page, Windows / Mac desktop clients and all services that link to this policy.

This policy distinguishes two usage modes: **Local Offline Account Mode** and **Cloud Account Mode**.

When you use Local Offline Account Mode, your account data and user files are stored locally on your NAS hardware. Our cloud servers do not collect or store your personal information.

This policy does not apply to third‑party applications, plugins and third‑party cloud services you install on your NAS device. Please refer to the privacy statements provided by those third parties.

## 2. Information We Collect

For detailed collection scenarios and fields, please refer to Personal Information Processing List. We will not collect information beyond the purposes stated in our documents without your explicit consent.

1) Information you actively provide: account registration information, feedback content, contact information, screenshots and descriptions submitted for technical support.

2) Information automatically collected when you use cloud services: device SN, model, firmware version, operation logs, crash logs, login records, IP and city‑level location parsed from IP.

3) Special note for overseas users: Users outside Mainland China register by email only. We do not collect phone numbers, advertising identifiers (OAID / GAID / IDFA), or precise GPS location data. Location‑related data is only temporarily used during device network setup to read Wi‑Fi name and derive country code and will not be uploaded or persisted.

4) Information stored on your NAS: Your photos, documents, contacts and other private files are stored locally on your NAS. Without your explicit authorization, we cannot read, access or upload such private file content.

## 3. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

1) Provide, maintain, optimize services: complete account login, device binding, remote‑link service operation, troubleshooting, product iteration and stability improvement.

2) Account and security protection: identify abnormal login, prevent unauthorized access, verify identity during password reset.

3) Customer service and technical support: process work orders, reply to your consultation and feedback.

4) Send necessary service notifications: device status alerts, security reminders, important service updates. Marketing emails for overseas users are only sent with your voluntary opt‑in.

5) Comply with legal obligations: respond to legal requirements, judicial or regulatory requests.

We will not use your personal information for purposes other than those stated in this policy without your separate consent.

## 4. Information Storage and Security

1) Storage location: Data of Mainland‑China cloud‑account users will be stored within Mainland China. For overseas cloud‑account users, data may be stored in overseas secure servers. Local‑account data remains entirely on your NAS hardware and will not be uploaded to our cloud.

2) Retention period: We retain cloud‑side personal data only for the period necessary to fulfil service purposes. After you cancel your account, we will delete or anonymize your cloud‑side account‑related data, unless legal regulations require retention.

3) Security safeguards: We adopt encryption for data in‑transit and at‑rest, access control and other technical and organizational measures to protect your cloud‑stored personal information. But please acknowledge that no internet security measure can guarantee absolute safety.

## 5. Information Sharing, Disclosure and Transfer

1) We will not sell your personal information to any third party.

2) Limited sharing with authorized third‑party service providers: Only necessary data will be shared with SDK / service providers listed in Third‑Party Personal Information Sharing List for completing corresponding functions. Third parties are prohibited from re‑using such data for other purposes.

3) Legal disclosure: We may disclose personal information when required by applicable laws, judicial orders or government regulatory requirements.

4) Business transfer: In case of merger, acquisition or asset transfer, personal information may be transferred as part of business assets; we will notify you before such transfer.

## 6. Your Rights Over Personal Information

Subject to applicable local laws, you may exercise the following rights via App / client settings:

1) Right to access: You may view your account‑related personal information.

2) Right to correction: You may correct inaccurate or incomplete account information.

3) Right to withdraw consent: You may revoke permission authorizations or marketing subscription. Revoking consent will not affect processing already performed based on prior consent.

4) Right to deletion / account cancellation: You may apply to cancel your cloud account. After cancellation, cloud‑side personal data will be deleted or anonymized as required by law.

For local‑account users: Most data resides on your NAS device. You may delete data through NAS device management functions; we cannot remotely delete data stored on your local NAS hardware.

## 7. Minors Protection

Our services are mainly intended for adults. If you are a minor, please use services under the guidance and consent of your parent or legal guardian. If we accidentally collect minor’s personal data, we will take measures to delete such data as soon as we become aware.

## 8. Cross‑border Transmission of Personal Information

When you use overseas cloud‑account services, your personal information may be transmitted and stored outside your home jurisdiction. We will take appropriate safeguards in compliance with applicable cross‑border data‑transfer regulations.

Local‑account data will not be uploaded to cloud servers, hence no cross‑border transmission occurs.

## 9. Revision of this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy according to legal updates and service adjustments. Updated text will be published on our policy page. For material changes affecting your rights, we will send advance notice via in‑app notice or email. Your continued use of services after effective date means you accept revised policy. If you disagree, please stop using services.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have inquiries, requests or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or personal‑information‑right exercise, please send email to service@spcnasos.com.

We will respond to your request within reasonable time required by applicable laws. If you are dissatisfied with our reply, you may submit complaints to local competent data‑protection authorities.

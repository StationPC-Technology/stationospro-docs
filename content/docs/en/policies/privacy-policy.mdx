Update Date Oct 19, 2026

Firefly Technology Co., Limited (hereinafter referred to as "we") values your personal information and the security of data on your NAS. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information when you use StationHub NAS devices, systems, clients, web management portals, remote connections, and related services.

StationHub is designed for home, team, and enterprise NAS scenarios. Different device models, system versions, regions, account types, and administrator settings may result in different processing activities; the descriptions on specific feature pages, in pop-ups, and in the [Personal Information Processing List](./personal-information-processing-list) have the same effect as this Policy. Before using StationHub, please read this Policy and the [User Agreement](./user-agreement).

**If you use StationHub on behalf of an enterprise, school, family, or other organization, you should confirm that you have obtained authorization and provide the necessary privacy notice to organization members. Organization administrators bear primary management responsibility for member accounts, shared content, and access permissions in the organizational space.**

## 1. Scope and Roles

1.1. This Policy applies to StationHub NAS devices and their systems, mobile and desktop clients, web management pages, account services, remote management, device discovery, updates, notifications, technical support, and services that link to this Policy.

1.2. This Policy does not apply to third-party applications, containers, plugins, cloud drives, AI services, or other external services that you install on the NAS. Such services process information independently by their providers; please refer to their privacy policies. Device administrators or enterprise organizations may establish additional data processing rules, which should be separately communicated to the relevant members.

1.3. In personal and home scenarios, you are generally the controller of your own data and the data of family members; in enterprise or other organizational scenarios, the organization generally determines the purposes and methods of processing member data, and we provide technical services in accordance with the service agreement between the organization and us or the administrator's instructions. We will not decide on the organization's behalf whether member data may be accessed, exported, or deleted.

## 2. What Information We Collect

We process information only to the extent necessary to provide services, ensure security, provide support, or fulfill legal obligations. The actual fields are subject to the feature pages and the Personal Information Processing List.

### 2.1 Account and Identity Information

This includes email address, mobile phone number (if supported in your region and by the feature), verification codes, password digests, account ID, nickname, avatar, organization ID, member role, device ownership relationship, and account security settings. When an enterprise administrator invites or removes members, we also process invitation relationships, roles, and operation records.

### 2.2 Device and Configuration Data

This includes device serial number, model, system and firmware version, device name, online status, storage and network configuration summaries, client type, IP address, region or country/region, remote connection status, and device information required to provide updates, warranty, device discovery, and remote management.

### 2.3 Operation, Security, and Audit Logs

This includes login time, login method, device and browser information, city-level location inferred from IP address, authentication failures, permission changes, remote access, member invitations, application installations, system updates, and error and crash logs. Enterprise features may provide audit records visible to administrators; administrators should explain the audit scope and retention period within the organization.

### 2.4 Feedback, Support, and Repair Data

This includes inquiries, work orders, contact information, problem descriptions, screenshots, diagnostic logs, device configurations, and files you proactively provide. We will request the minimum information possible; if files or configurations need to be temporarily read for troubleshooting, we will first explain the scope to you and obtain the necessary authorization.

### 2.5 Optional Features and Third-Party Connection Information

When you enable remote access, cloud sync, push notifications, the app center, backup, third-party cloud drives, containers, or AI services, we may process the corresponding tokens, domain names, task statuses, file paths, thumbnails, model or application names, and transmission logs. The content actually read and processed by third-party services is the responsibility of the third party you select under its own rules.

### 2.6 NAS Local File Content

In the case of local management, LAN access, and cloud features not enabled, photos, documents, databases, backups, contacts, and other file content are stored on your NAS or terminal device, and we will not automatically read, copy, or upload them through background processes. To enable remote access, sync, search, thumbnails, AI, or technical support features that you actively enable, the relevant content may be processed in accordance with the page descriptions.

## 3. How We Use Personal Information

3.1. To provide registration, login, device binding, member management, permission control, and NAS management features.

3.2. To establish remote connections, send device alerts and security notifications, and provide system updates, device discovery, warranty, and technical support.

3.3. To detect abnormal logins, abuse, malware, and network attacks, record audit events, and protect the security of StationHub, users, and third parties.

3.4. To analyze crashes and performance issues, improve systems and clients, and compile service usage statistics. Optional analytics or experience surveys will be separately prompted or consented to when required.

3.5. To respond to your requests, handle complaints, perform contracts, and comply with laws, regulations, judicial, or regulatory requirements. If we need to use information for purposes not described in this Policy, we will inform you through appropriate means and obtain authorization when required.

## 4. Data Processing in Home, Enterprise, and Organizational Spaces

4.1. The owner of a home device can create members, share directories, and manage device permissions. The owner should explain the scope of sharing to family members and is responsible for invitations, permission settings, and remote access configurations.

4.2. Enterprises or other organizations may designate administrators to manage member accounts, shared spaces, applications, backups, and audit logs. Administrators may access or export organizational data in accordance with organizational policies and may apply to deactivate members. We provide services according to the organization's instructions but are not responsible for verifying whether the organization's internal authorization is lawful.

4.3. If enterprise members need to access, correct, export, or delete personal information in the organizational space, they should first contact the organization administrator; if the request involves account information or security logs independently controlled by us, they may also contact us through the method in Section 12 of this Policy. Data processing after an organization member leaves, changes positions, or the organizational space is dissolved shall be governed by the organization's policies, the service agreement, and applicable law.

## 5. Information Storage, Cross-Border Transfer, and Retention Periods

5.1. NAS local accounts, local device configurations, and file content for which cloud features are not enabled are primarily stored on your device or terminal. Information required for cloud accounts, remote connections, notifications, updates, and support may be stored on servers operated by us or our service providers, subject to the feature descriptions and applicable law.

5.2. To provide cross-regional remote access, account security, and cloud services, necessary information may be transferred to servers outside your country or region. We will adopt encryption, de-identification, access control, contractual constraints, or other safeguards in accordance with applicable law. Before enabling cross-border sync or third-party cloud services, you should confirm their data transfer arrangements.

5.3. We will retain personal information for the period necessary to achieve the processing purposes; after account cancellation, device unbinding, closure of the organizational space, or withdrawal of your consent, we will delete, anonymize, or stop processing the relevant information, except where retention is required by laws and regulations, dispute resolution, security auditing, or backup recovery. Data in backups may be overwritten after a reasonable period.

## 6. Information Sharing and Third-Party Processing

6.1. We do not sell your personal information. To provide hosting, messaging, troubleshooting, customer service, payment, cybersecurity, data storage, or remote connection services, we may provide necessary information to bound service providers and require them to process it only for the agreed purposes.

6.2. In enterprise scenarios, organization administrators and service personnel designated by the organization may receive member accounts, permissions, audits, and organizational data. The organization bears the corresponding notification and management responsibilities for such access.

6.3. Third-party applications, cloud drives, containers, or AI services that you actively connect to will process data in accordance with the permissions you grant. We do not control third parties' privacy practices and are not responsible for third parties' processing results merely because we provide an integration entry point.

6.4. We may disclose relevant information when necessary for laws and regulations, judicial proceedings, regulatory requirements, protection of users or public safety, handling infringement complaints, or safeguarding our lawful rights and interests. In the event of a merger, acquisition, asset transfer, or change of service entity, we will notify you in accordance with law and require the transferee to continue to fulfill the corresponding protection obligations.

## 7. Cookies, Analytics, and Notifications

7.1. The web management portal may use necessary cookies or similar technologies to maintain login, language, and security status. Disabling necessary cookies may cause some features to be unavailable.

7.2. We may use de-identified device and service metrics to analyze stability, performance, and security risks. Features involving identifiable personal information or non-essential analytics will provide an option to disable or withdraw consent in appropriate scenarios.

7.3. We will send necessary notifications related to account security, device status, system updates, service changes, and work orders. Marketing or experience survey information will be sent with consent when required by applicable law, and you may unsubscribe in the manner described in the notification.

## 8. Information Security

We adopt measures such as access control, identity verification, encryption in transit and at rest, least privilege, log auditing, vulnerability remediation, backup, and personnel management to protect personal information. Because the internet, terminal devices, and users' network environments are never absolutely secure, we cannot guarantee that all risks can be eliminated. Please use strong passwords, enable available multi-factor authentication, limit the number of administrators, update the system promptly, and do not submit unnecessary sensitive information in work orders or chats.

If a personal information security incident that may affect your rights and interests occurs, we will take remedial measures in accordance with applicable law and notify affected users or organizations through in-app notifications, email, announcements, or other appropriate means.

## 9. How You Can Manage Personal Information

Subject to applicable law and service conditions, you may:

- access, copy, or correct account, member, and device information;
- manage notifications, system permissions, remote access, device analytics, and marketing subscriptions;
- apply to delete your account, unbind devices, delete cloud data, or withdraw optional authorizations;
- apply to export data from your personal account or from the organizational space that you are entitled to export;
- raise questions or requests for restriction regarding automated processing, cross-border transfers, or third-party connections.

Personal accounts can be managed through StationHub's account or device settings; organization members should contact the organization administrator first. We may verify your identity, account ownership, or organizational authorization before processing a request. To fulfill legal obligations, protect the rights of others, maintain security, or handle disputes, we may be unable to fully satisfy all requests immediately, and we will explain the applicable reasons.

## 10. Minors and Family Members

StationHub is primarily intended for adults and organizational users. Minors should use it with the consent and guidance of their parents or other legal guardians. Home device owners must not collect, share, or publicly disclose minors' personal information without the necessary authorization. If you believe that we have processed a minor's information without the guardian's consent, please notify us through the contact method in Section 12.

## 11. Boundaries of Third-Party Applications and External Services

Third-party applications, containers, plugins, cloud drives, and AI services installed on the NAS may read files, network, or account information on the device. Before enabling them, please review their permissions and privacy policies, and complete approval in enterprise environments. Uninstalling a third-party application does not necessarily delete data it has already written to the NAS, external storage, or third-party servers; you should handle such data according to the procedures of the corresponding service provider.

## 12. Policy Updates and Contact Us

12.1. We will update this Policy in response to changes in law, technology, and services. Material changes will be explained through the website, in-app notifications, email, or other appropriate means; after the updated Policy is published, your continued use of the relevant services constitutes acceptance of the updated content, unless laws require us to obtain consent again.

12.2. If you have questions, requests, or complaints regarding this Policy, personal information processing, or data security, please send an email to service@spcnasos.com. We will process your request within the period required by applicable law; if you are dissatisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

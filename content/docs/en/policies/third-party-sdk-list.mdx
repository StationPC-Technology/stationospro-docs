Update Date Sep 29, 2026

To deliver complete and stable functionalities for StationPC, we integrate third-party components including SDKs, application programming interfaces (APIs), and plugins supplied by external partners. This document lists relevant third-party partners. Please review each third-party’s privacy policy for full details on how they handle personal information.

Please be aware that third-party components may adjust their data-processing scope due to version iterations or policy revisions. Please refer to official announcements released by respective third-party service providers.

Scope: StationPC StationNAS App (Android / iOS)

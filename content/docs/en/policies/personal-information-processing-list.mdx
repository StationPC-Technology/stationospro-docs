This list divides StationHub processing in mobile, web, and PC clients into “trigger — information — storage location” so you can see whether a feature may use network transmission. When cloud capabilities are off, local accounts and NAS files remain primarily on the device.

## 1. Accounts and membership

| Trigger | Purpose | Information that may be involved | Main location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Create a local offline account | Manage a device without a cloud account | Local account and password | User NAS |
| Enable two-step verification for a local account | Send a code and protect the account | Verification email and code status | User NAS |
| Register or sign in to a cloud account | Verify identity and manage the account | Phone number in Mainland China; email elsewhere; code, password, nickname, avatar, account ID | Cloud account service |
| Recover a password or verify ownership | Handle account-security requests | Account details and device SN | Account service and security logs |
| Invite a member or join an organization | Manage shared devices and folders | Organization ID, role, device ownership, invitation and permission records | Device or organization service |

## 2. Device access and network services

| Action | Purpose | Information that may be involved | Main location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bind, unbind, or manage a NAS | Establish the device relationship and provide operations | Device SN, model, firmware, MAC, IP, name, status, warranty information | Device and cloud device service |
| Search the local network | Find available devices on the same network | Name, IP, MAC, SN, firmware, status, Wake-on-LAN status | Usually temporary on the local network |
| View device status | Provide access management and operating status | Online, offline, or sleep state; owner/shared-user role | Device or device service |
| Use remote connections | Access or manage a device from outside | Remote ID, device SN, IP, router port, domain, connection status | Remote-connection service and necessary logs |
| Back up or restore configuration | Migrate, repair, or restore a device | User permissions, file, network, security, and system settings | NAS or backup location selected by you |

## 3. Operation, analytics, and security

| Trigger | Purpose | Information that may be involved | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crash, error, or performance fault | Diagnose issues and improve stability | Crash logs, device model, system/software version, operation logs | OAID, GAID, and IDFA are not collected |
| Product analytics | Understand feature use and in-app paths | Account ID, user type, device name/SN/model, app and system version, feature events | Processed by analytics providers when enabled |
| Sign-in and security audit | Detect unusual sign-ins and unauthorized access | Time, IP, city-level location, system type, sign-in method, operation records | Security logs |
| Trusted-device management | Reduce repeated verification and protect accounts | Trusted device model, sign-in place/time, device list | Account or device service |
| Updates, notices, and operations | Send status, backup, security, and update reminders | Device status, task progress, version, necessary notification identifiers | Device, notification, or account service |

## 4. Support, app center, and third-party connections

| Trigger | Purpose | Information that may be involved | Storage or handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support, troubleshooting, or repair | Locate issues and provide assistance | Contact details, device SN, operation/error logs, network information, screenshots and descriptions you submit | Only within the submitted or authorized scope |
| Overseas ticket or technical request | Handle an issue outside Mainland China | Region, device SN, description, optional nickname and device type | Used by the support process |
| Match an app in the app center | Provide compatible third-party apps | Device SN, model, firmware, IP | Sent to services needed by the app center |
| Report or appeal about a third-party app | Maintain the app ecosystem | App name, description, screenshots, email | Handled by support and app administrators |
| Connect a third-party cloud | Sync NAS and cloud files | Temporary third-party token and task status | Token kept only as needed; deletion follows that service’s rules |
| Back up and restore phone contacts | Keep contacts on your own NAS | Name, phone, email, group | Stored only on the NAS you select |

## 5. Optional research and device improvement

| Participation | Purpose | Information that may be involved | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voluntary overseas experience research | Collect feedback and improve products | Email and cloud account voluntarily provided | Outside Mainland China |
| Voluntary device-improvement plan | Analyze usage and stability | Account/device ID, IP, behavior statistics, crash logs, location and network information | Mainland China, only after the page invitation |

## 6. Boundaries for files and location

1. Local offline accounts do not upload personal data to the cloud; the data remains on the user’s NAS.
2. Without your active authorization, we do not read, scan, parse, or upload private NAS files.
3. We do not collect or retain precise location. During setup, we may temporarily read the Wi-Fi name and derive a country code; coordinates are not uploaded or stored persistently.
4. Advertising identifiers are not collected outside Mainland China. Crash diagnostics and product analytics follow the scenarios in this list.
5. You can view, correct, or delete information under your control in App settings and request account closure. Organization-space requests may require contacting the administrator first.

Read this list together with the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy), [Permission Request and Usage Statement](./permission-usage-statement), and [Third-Party Personal Information Sharing List](./third-party-sdk-list). We will update it when features, components, or legal requirements change.

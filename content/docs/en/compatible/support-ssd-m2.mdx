## Hardware Compatibility Notes
All models listed in the table below have been verified by the StationPC Lab and can be used with confidence. The DA400 series is inherently compatible with the vast majority of drive models on the market — a model not appearing in the list does not mean it is unsupported. This compatibility list is updated from time to time as we continue testing and adding more drive models.

1. The M.2 slot supports NVMe-protocol SSDs in the 2280 form factor.
2. Due to PCIe 4.0 compatibility and power consumption considerations, high-power PCIe 4.0 SSDs are not recommended.
3. The M.2 slot is backward compatible with PCIe 3.0 SSDs (running at PCIe 3.0 speeds), which typically also consume less power and generate less heat.
4. When configuring high-speed storage, the SSD capacity must be no less than 32GB, with a maximum supported capacity of 8TB.
5. Choose an SSD from a well-known major brand.
6. SSDs with heatsink armor are not supported; if your SSD has a thick label on the back, it is recommended to peel it off before installation.

Note: An SSD's lifespan is limited by its number of write cycles. Whenever possible, choose a model with higher endurance to reduce potential risks.

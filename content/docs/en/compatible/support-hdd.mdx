## Hardware Compatibility Notes
All models listed in the table below have been verified by the StationPC Lab and can be used with confidence. The DA400 series is inherently compatible with the vast majority of drive models on the market — a model not appearing in the list does not mean it is unsupported. This compatibility list is updated from time to time as we continue testing and adding more drive models.

1. **Drive bays and interfaces**: The DA400 comes with 4 SATA drive bays, supporting 3.5-inch HDDs as well as 2.5-inch SATA SSDs; SAS-interface drives are not currently supported.
2. **Capacity per drive**: Approximately 100GB minimum (corresponding to drives with a market-labeled capacity of 120GB or above), and up to 32TB maximum.
3. **Avoid SMR (shingled magnetic recording) drives**: SMR drives deliver unstable write performance and are especially not recommended for building RAID 1 / RAID 5 arrays. To find out whether a drive is SMR-based, check with your dealer or look up the specific model online.
4. **RAID recommendations**: When building a RAID 5 array, enterprise-class or NAS drives are recommended; otherwise the potential risk to your data is considerably higher.
5. **Proprietary brand-customized drives**: Drives custom-built for certain brands are not recommended, as they may fail to be detected or exhibit unexplained anomalies.
6. **Recommended drives**: Major-brand NAS-class drives are recommended, such as the Seagate IronWolf series and the Western Digital Red Plus / Pro series.

<Callout title="Tip" type="idea">
3.5-inch and 2.5-inch drives use different mounting screws. For installation instructions, refer to the [DA400 Installation Guide](../quick-start/da400-install).
</Callout>

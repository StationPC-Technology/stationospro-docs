## Specifications
* Protocol Requirement: Supports PD Protocol
* Output Parameters: 12V 2.5A and above (Minimum Power ≥30W)

## Notes
1. Using adapters that do not meet the above specifications may lead to insufficient power supply for the Dock, causing issues such as unstable device connection, abnormal data transmission, and failure of extended functions.
2. Non-original adapters must strictly match the required protocol and power parameters to avoid device damage or poor user experience due to insufficient power supply.
3. To confirm the compatibility of third-party power adapters, you can verify against the above specifications or contact us at support@stationpc.com.

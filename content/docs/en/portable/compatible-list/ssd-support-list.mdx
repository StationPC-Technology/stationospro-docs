PocketCloud SSD Compatibility Notes:
* Specifications: Only supports M.2 NVMe 2280;
* Protocol & Speed: Compatible with PCIe 3.0/4.0 protocols; actual operating speed is PCIe 3.0 x2;
* Power Supply : 3.3V, maximum support for 3A power supply;
* Capacity Support: The maximum capacity is 8TB, and larger capacities are theoretically supported; some tested models of over 8TB from certain brands can also work normally. For details, refer to the compatibility list;
* Installation & Heat Dissipation: SSDs with heat sinks need to have the heat sink removed before installation (applicable to both the host and dock); the host is equipped with an active cooling fan, and the dock comes with a built-in heat sink. No additional cooling accessories are required for long-term stable operation.

title: "Client Unable to Connect to Device"
---

<Tabs items={["Mobile","PC"]}>

<Tab>

## LAN Connection

- Make sure the device has been configured for network access, and that your phone and the device are on the same LAN

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/lan-2.png" class="responsive-img-45" />

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/lan-1.png" class="responsive-img-45" />

- Enter the correct device IP address, account, and password to log in

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/lan-3.png" class="responsive-img-30" />

## Direct Connection

- Make sure the device has entered direct connection mode, and that your phone is connected to the hotspot network generated by the device

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/ap-1.png" class="responsive-img-45" />

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/ap-2.png" class="responsive-img-45" />

- Enter the correct device IP address (fixed at 192.168.88.1), account, and password to log in

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/ap-3.png" class="responsive-img-30" />

## Remote Connection

- Make sure the device has been configured for network access, can access the external network normally, and the side indicator is blue

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/lan-2.png" class="responsive-img-45" />

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/id-2.png" class="responsive-img-45" />

- Make sure remote access has been enabled on the device

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/id-1.png" class="responsive-img-30" />

- Enter the correct Station ID, account, and password to log in

<img src="/images/common/portable/troubleshooting/client-connect-issue/id-3.png" class="responsive-img-30" />

## Other Notes

- If the connection fails, troubleshoot each access method step by step and make sure every step is completed correctly
- If the issue persists, submit feedback with logs and [contact customer support](/portable/contact/contact)

</Tab>

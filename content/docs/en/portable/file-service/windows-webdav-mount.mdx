title: "Mounting WebDAV on a Local Computer in Windows"
---

1. Click the Star button in the bottom left-hand corner of your screen.

<img src="/images/common/portable/file-service/windows-webdav-mount/1-1.png" width="10%" />

2. Type "This PC" and you will see the following screen.

<img src="/images/common/portable/file-service/windows-webdav-mount/1-2.png" width="40%" />

3. Choose "Map Network Drive" from the quick menu on the right of the screen.

<img src="/images/common/portable/file-service/windows-webdav-mount/1-3.png" width="40%" />

4. In the pop-up window, enter the WebDAV address, which is `http://:5005`, and then click **Finish**

<img src="/images/common/portable/file-service/windows-webdav-mount/1-4.png" width="40%" />

5. When prompted, enter your WebDAV username and password and press **OK**.
6. Verify that the process is complete by going back to 'This PC'. The mapped letter drive should appear in the menu under the "Network Location" section.

title: "Garbled Chinese Characters When Accessing an NFS Disk from Windows"
---

> Garbled Chinese characters are usually caused by an encoding mismatch or a system locale issue. For the problem of garbled Chinese characters on a mapped NFS disk in Windows, testing shows that the following method resolves it:

> Steps:

1. Open **Control Panel** → **Clock and Region** → **Region**.
2. Switch to the **Administrative** tab and click **Change system locale**.
3. Make sure **"Chinese (Simplified, China)"** is selected. ```Tick the Beta option at the bottom```
<img src="/images/en/portable/file-service/windows-nfs-garbled-code/1-1.png" width="100%" height="100%" />
4. Restart the system after the change, mount the NFS disk again, and check whether Chinese characters are displayed correctly.

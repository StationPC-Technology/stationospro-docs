title: "Some Issues with USB Drive Mode"
---

### 1. What is the transfer speed of USB Drive Mode?
The theoretical transfer speed of USB Drive Mode is approximately 1000MB/s.

### 2. Why can't iPhone use USB Drive Mode?
Currently, only iPhone 15 and later models with Type-C ports support access to USB Drive Mode.

### 3. Can encrypted space be accessed in USB Drive Mode?
No, it cannot. Encrypted space can only be used via APP login.

### 4. Why is file transfer slow in USB Drive Mode?
Please use the official matching cable or a standard USB 3.0 cable, and ensure connection to a USB 3.0 or higher port.

title: "How to Submit Feedback for PocketCloud?"
---

1. On the APP side, click **User > Feedback**;  

2. Follow the on-screen prompts to fill in the relevant information (such as issue description, suggestions, etc.), then submit once completed.  

> Warm Tip: When submitting feedback, it is recommended to check the box for "Log Reporting" — the logs only contain debugging data needed for issue localization, and **do NOT involve your file content or personal privacy information**. This helps the technical team troubleshoot and resolve issues more quickly.

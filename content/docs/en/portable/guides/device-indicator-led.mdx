title: "Indicator Lights Guide"
---

The indicator lights of PocketCloud are located on the side of the device. Different light statuses correspond to different operating states of the device, with details as follows:

| Indicator Light Status       | Device Status               |
| :--------------------------- | :-------------------------- |
| Solid Orange Light           | Internet not connected |
| Solid Blue Light             | Internet connected |
| Blinking Orange or Blue Light| Charging |
| All Lights Off               | Sleep mode or Powered off |

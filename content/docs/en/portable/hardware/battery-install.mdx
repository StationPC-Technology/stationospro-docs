When the batteries age, they can be replaced. The operation is as 
follows:

## Operation Steps

1. Prepare two brand-new, unused 18650 (3.6/3.7V) batteries of the same model from a reputable/commonly available brand. A capacity of over 3000mAh is recommended.
Do not use damaged or swollen batteries. Mixing new and old batteries is prohibited.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/battery-install/battery-install_1.jpg)

2. Ensure the device is powered off and the indicator light is off.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/battery-install/battery-install_2.jpg)

3. Using the screwdriver proceed to loosen the screws and remove the cover.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/battery-install/battery-install_3.jpg)

4. Push the battery cover open in the direction of the arrow. Grab the PocketCloud with two hands, as shown in the illustration below. Then, push it forward with your thumbs.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/battery-install/battery-install_4.jpg)

5. Insert new batteries with correct polarity, close the battery cover.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/battery-install/battery-install_5.jpg)

6. Replace the cover and tighten the screws.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/battery-install/battery-install_6.jpg)

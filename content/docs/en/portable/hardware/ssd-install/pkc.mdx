You can install one M.2 NVMe 2280 SSD on your device.

## Operation Steps

1. Ensure the device is powered off and the indicator light is off.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/ssd-install/pkc/pkc-ssd-install_1.png)

2. Using the screwdriver to loosen the screws and remove.
the cover.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/ssd-install/pkc/pkc-ssd-install_2.png)

3. Insert the M.2 NVMe SSD into the slot and tighten the screw.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/ssd-install/pkc/pkc-ssd-install_3.png)

4. Replace the cover and tighten the screws.

![](/images/common/portable/hardware/ssd-install/pkc/pkc-ssd-install_4.png)

Factory reset allows you to restore the device to its initial state, and the operation must be performed via the APP.  

> Note: If the device is bound to a remote service, factory reset will automatically unbind the service, and an internet connection is required to complete the operation.

## How to Factory Reset
1. Open the client, go to the **Devices** page, and find the **Hardware & System > Factory Reset** option;  
2. Tap **Factory Reset**, then enter the password to initiate the reset process. 
 
> * Hard Drive Formatting: If you select "Do Not Format", the system will automatically create an "olduserdata" directory. After the reset is completed, the original data files will be saved in this directory.

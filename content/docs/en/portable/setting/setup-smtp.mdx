The email notification service enables the system to send emails, including two-factor authentication (2FA) emergency verification code emails and password reset emails.

> Supported email providers: Gmail, Yahoo, Apple, QQ, 163, and custom SMTP servers.

## Enable Email Notification Service
1. You can enable the service at **Device > Notifications > Email Notification**.

2. Select an email service provider based on your actual needs, obtain the corresponding authorization code following the instructions below, and submit to complete the setup.

3. After setup, we recommend running a send test to confirm the email service has been successfully authorized.

## How to Obtain an Email Authorization Code
### Gmail
1. Go to [myaccount.google.com](myaccount.google.com) to access your Google Account management page. Click the search icon, enter **App passwords**, and select the search result to navigate to the settings page.
<img src="/images/common/portable/setting/setup-smtp/2-1-1.png" width="100%" />

2. Enter a custom app name and click **Create** to generate your authorization code.
<img src="/images/common/portable/setting/setup-smtp/2-1-2.png" width="100%" />

### Apple

1. Visit the official Apple ID website: [appleid.apple.com ](appleid.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple ID.

2. Go to Sign-In and Security > App-Specific Passwords.
<img src="/images/common/portable/setting/setup-smtp/2-2-1.png" width="100%" />

3. Click Generate an app-specific password.
<img src="/images/common/portable/setting/setup-smtp/2-2-2.png" width="100%" />

4. Enter a label to name this password (e.g. StationPC) and click Create.

### Yahoo

1. Sign in to your [Yahoo Account Security page](https://login.yahoo.com/?done=https%3A%2F%2Flogin.yahoo.com%2Fmyaccount%2Fsecurity%3F.lang%3Den-US%26.intl%3Dus%26.src%3Dyhelp%26.scrumb%3D0&src=mc&.intl=us&.lang=en-US).
2. Under "External connections," click Create app password.
3. Enter your app's name in the text field.
4. Click Generate password.
5. Use the one-time password to log in to your 3rd party app.

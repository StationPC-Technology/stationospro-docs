The Monitor app tracks hardware resources and service processes in real time, helping you understand system status and locate performance bottlenecks.

## Status Monitoring

The **Status** page provides overview and per-resource views. The top sub-tabs include:

- **Overview**
- **CPU**
- **VPU**
- **Memory**
- **Networks**
- **NPU**
- **AI Module Expansion**

### Overview

The overview page shows in real time:

- **Fan speed**: current fan RPM;
- **CPU**: processor temperature and utilization;
- **Memory**: available capacity and usage;
- **Networks**: send and receive speeds;
- **NPU**: utilization of the AI processing unit;
- **Hard disk**: read and write speeds.

Click **Details** in the upper-right corner of a card to view live charts and more information for that resource.

![Monitor Status](/images/common/app-guide/monitor-img/status.png)

### Hard Drive Information

The **Hard Drive Information** table at the bottom lists each disk's number, built-in name, temperature, busy rate, read/write speeds, and purpose (the storage volume it belongs to) — a quick way to check disk health and load.

## Process Manager

The **Process Manager** page has two sub-tabs:

### Service

Displays system services (such as Log Center, Settings, App Center, Docker, Monitor, and Storage) and their child processes in a tree, showing:

- **CPU Occupancy**
- **Running Time**
- **Memory**

Click a column header to sort. Expand a service to inspect its child processes.

![Process Service](/images/common/app-guide/monitor-img/process-service.png)

### Process

Lists all system processes with PID, name, CPU, memory, and running time to further pinpoint resource usage.

![Process List](/images/common/app-guide/monitor-img/process-list.png)

<Callout title="Caution" type="warn">
  Terminating critical system processes may cause service failures or data corruption. When in doubt, restart the service instead or contact technical support.
</Callout>
